The depot keeps one running note for every shift, and the note is written by the lead on duty.
A note names the hour it opened, the hour it closed, and the person who signed it off.
Goods that arrive without a docket wait in the amber bay until a docket is found or written.
A pallet that stands in the amber bay for more than two shifts goes back to the sender.
The forklift log is signed at the start of each shift and again when the battery is changed.
A battery that reads under a quarter is swapped rather than charged in place.
The cold room door is checked twice a shift, and the reading is written on the door card.
A reading outside the range on the card is reported to the shift lead at once.
Cardboard is flattened before it reaches the baler, and the baler is emptied before the last hour.
The yard gate is locked by the lead on duty, and the key returns to the board in the office.
Visitors sign the book at the front desk and wear a badge until they leave.
A delivery booked for the morning that has not arrived by noon is chased by the desk.
The desk keeps the chase note with the booking, so the next shift can read what was said.
Orders picked but not dispatched are left on the outbound rack with their paperwork on top.
Nothing leaves the outbound rack without a signature from the driver who takes it.
