tag checks each label in your list against a piece of text and prints every label that fits, separated by tabs. A text may get several labels, one, or none.

<Example id="tag-both" />

Both labels: the message is about a double charge and a late parcel.

## One label

<Example id="tag-one" />

billing only: the message asks for money back and says nothing about a delivery.

## No label

<Example id="tag-none" />

No label fits, so the answer is empty.

## See why

<details class="example-disclosure">
<summary>See why</summary>

`--json` prints the details. This `jq` keeps the chance of each label.

<Example id="tag-why" />

tag keeps each label whose chance reaches the bar, 0.5 by default. Both 0.9 and 0.8 reach it. [JSON output](/reference/json-output/) lists every field.

</details>

## Move the bar

<details class="example-disclosure">
<summary>Move the bar</summary>

Raise the bar to 0.85 and only billing is kept, because shipping has 0.8.

<Example id="tag-bar" />

Lower the bar to give more labels. Raise it to give only the clear ones.

</details>
