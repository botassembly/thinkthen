ThinkThen answers a question about a piece of text, such as whether a customer's message asks for a refund. It gives an answer your script or program can act on: yes or no, one of your choices, a score, or not sure when the text could go either way.

## Pick the function that fits your question

- [decide](/functions/decide/): yes, no or not sure.
- [choose](/functions/choose/): one of your choices, or not sure.
- [filter](/functions/filter/): keeps the lines that answer yes.
- [tag](/functions/tag/): every label that fits.
- [score](/functions/score/): a number from 0 to 1 along your levels.
- [rank](/functions/rank/): your lines in order along your levels.
- [find](/functions/find/): the one line that answers, or none.
- [recognize](/functions/recognize/): the names of the kinds you ask for.
- [relate](/functions/relate/): how those names connect.
- [annotate](/functions/annotate/): several questions about each line at once.

The same ten functions work from the command line, from thirteen programming languages, and inside DuckDB, SQLite and PostgreSQL.
