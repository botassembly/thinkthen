## What preparation taught us

A first candidate would have replaced the two quiet command sockets with `Listener::serving(Vec::new())` and `connections() == 0`. Source inspection rejected it before code: `conformance/backend/src/lifetime.rs:74-87` accepts on a background thread, and `listener.rs:318-326` increments the count only after that accept. A child could exit while its connection still waits in the kernel queue, so the new zero count would be weaker than the old synchronous `accept()`. `count()` is weaker still because it counts complete requests, and `requests()` drains observations. The selected positive-response group avoids that race: each caller observes a completed answer or after-write notification before checking counts. `Listener::serving` handles one response per connection and its final owner joins the acceptor and workers (`listener.rs:176-203,306-311`; `lifetime.rs:188-218`). The retained raw-byte, quiet, arrival and held-reply sites do not earn substitution merely because a `Canned` reply has a similar name. A fresh reviewer should accept or reject this exact one-helper group before runtime files are claimed.
