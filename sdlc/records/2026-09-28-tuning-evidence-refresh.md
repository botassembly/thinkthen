## Limits on the measurements and public carriers

- Experiment 297's chain comparison is `22/60` direct, `48/60` first hop, `51/60` two-hop, and `52/60` true-first-hop ceiling for that album-year cohort. `pipeline.py` skips a second-hop row when the first-hop answer has no usable album; its saved result and key contain **59** rows, so shipped audit's `0.864` uses 59, not 60. A paired audit comparison must use those same 59 case IDs and report the full 60-case summary separately. Thirteen distinct album inputs allowed reuse in that retained run. Neither equal cost nor a free second hop follows for arbitrary inputs, addresses, models, batch settings or cold caches. The source demo16 demonstrates a different pipeline.
- `runs/noise-summary.json` has 50 cases and three repetitions each, one case with an answer flip, median probability spread `0.020`, maximum `0.070`. Its `live_requests: 710` is the experiment ledger at that stage, not a noise-only count. The issue's “150 live requests” describes the 150 repeat observations, and should not be used as a transport-send oracle under default packing. The 517-example, ten-run external judge comparison added later is a different cohort and protocol; its percentages do not establish a ThinkThen repeat threshold.
- `runs/labels.json` holds a 228-question yes/no pool, fixed held 76, label pool 152 and 300 trials. The 10/30/60 uncertainty-versus-random table is conditional on that cohort, split, scoring and cut-tuning experiment. It motivates an order rule but cannot be an empirical pass/fail threshold for the new command. The original issue's requested advantage remains visible, with that limit.
- The per-case issue's older 989-call/$0.042 prose is historical. The retained corrected `experiments/297-gepa-loop-tests/LESSONS.md` totals experiments 296 and 297 as 1,049 live calls, 1,019,902 input tokens and about $0.043 on that accounting basis. Preserve both records and use the corrected final ledger for future cost claims. `--facts` reports whole-run requests/tokens when known; its totals cannot be assigned to individual saved case rows. Raw `--details` may hold `meta.usage`, but the proposed reader must inspect actual presence and type instead of inventing tokens.
- The `e8789b76` additions ask for miss shape, options in sent order, criterion-level selection and top-two evidence; `04d150b3` adds the separate external flip-rate comparison to repeat. A result's `input` and option order are available only where the saved detail row actually carries them; `Answer` does not by itself preserve all raw fields. `tag`/`annotate` member identity needs record id plus label/question name. Top-two is meaningful for a suitable choose distribution, not every verb. Group yes/no recall and confusion totals already exist; row-level shape and ranking do not.
