## What the preparation taught us

The most actionable observed gap is **10's first-use binding**, but 0228 is narrower than the register: cancellation, deadline, send-budget and other admitted-but-unsent paths can still publish a marker. The original issue also asks for a binding status and useful unbind guidance; neither is proved by the before-key exception. Keep issue/register 10 open after 0228 until the coordinator reviews every remaining criterion. An absent `--used` control in 0229 and a present empty manifest are likewise different states; do not let a parser's zero items bypass its required control check, as the recent C empty `call:{}` guard did. Finding 09's alias read-side suggestion cannot identify today's provider version from yesterday's stored reply. Finding 40's settled page and finding 105's reproducible fixture contract remain separate from their unbuilt warnings. No runtime, filesystem fixture or provider proof ran in this preparation.
