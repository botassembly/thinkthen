## What the preparation taught us

The most actionable observed gap is **10's first-use binding**, but publishing later must still put a marker ahead of a final entry and keep concurrent first writers from crossing addresses. A ticket should pin the absent-key case above and distinguish a request never prepared, a prepared request that fails before sending, and a cache hit against an unmarked legacy folder. A cache hit against a new empty folder is impossible because that folder has no entry to hit. The absent-key folder side effect is source-backed but lacks a current outside-in assertion; the proposed edge supplies that proof. The ticket should not combine this with revealing the bound address until the marker migration and library message receive review. Finding 09's alias read-side suggestion cannot identify today's provider version from yesterday's stored reply. Finding 40's settled page and finding 105's reproducible fixture contract must remain separate from their unbuilt warnings. No runtime, filesystem fixture or provider proof ran in this preparation.
