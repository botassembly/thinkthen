## What the build taught us

Preparation rejected the attractive quiet-socket conversion before source changes: an acceptor-thread counter can lag a child that already opened a socket. The positive-response tests have completed-answer and notification boundaries, so the shared fixture can preserve their relevant order. The shared listener's last-owner drop also closes the port before the replay-only call, making the no-live-backend part explicit. The earlier engine audit still found no safe whole-test deletion; ten other historical listener sites remain for separate equivalence review. This bounded cleanup does not close ticket 0119 or the umbrella harness issue.
