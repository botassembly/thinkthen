## What preparation taught us

The old issue correctly named three copied-payload guards but predated the Linux C++ fix. Source-only catch counts miss the earlier hook and separate worker thread. The first draft also treated payload Drop as safe, Linux SQLite proof as all-platform, and a Rust hook as necessarily outliving its DSO. The local artifact and one bounded unload probe made the residency decision concrete without editing product source. Review should check that these corrections avoid a secondary unwind and an unapproved host-visible pin while preserving explicit unavailable-platform gaps.
