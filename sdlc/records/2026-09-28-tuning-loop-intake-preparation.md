## Preparation lessons

The exchange filename is an opaque digest, even though an inspected entry contains a URL. Internal consistency does not prove historic origin or trusted custody of a writable folder. Whole-run `--facts` is newer than the filed cost premise; row-level usage and the monthly advisory file are different totals. A stage or pair digest is not a whole-plan identity. Source paths and current binary behavior govern older experimental prose. Review the exact diagnostic and persistence consumers before calling a proposed output safe. No six-report pair is an exact duplicate: each retains at least one criterion not supplied by its nearest existing issue or command.
