## Narrow panic correction for the same reviewer

At main `4935fb8c`, `libraries/python/src/worker.rs::run` catches work panic as `None`; `wait` maps it to Defect. ADR 0089 promises facts for returned started `Error` and terminal `Batch`, and explicitly permits observer panic to unwind after join. It supplies no final account on that unwind. Verify ADR 0091 now distinguishes a safe `panicked` Defect outcome with `facts=None` and `details=None` for every caught worker panic, even if a carrier was frozen before unwind. Ordinary post-call conversion errors retain that carrier through `Result`. No zero or partial snapshot is labelled final. This is a correction to the proposed receipt semantics, not a second Ian choice or a runtime claim. The earlier three review findings remain addressed.
