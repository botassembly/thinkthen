## What the build taught us

A permission probe must not close a raw descriptor on a database inode while SQLite connections may hold POSIX locks. SQLite-managed opens and an explicit read-only check preserve the pre-send refusal without disturbing its locks. Read-snapshot validation and an operation-owned connection remove repeated admission work while retaining external-change detection.

New-file creation has the same lock-loss hazard once its inode becomes visible. Guarding only the creator leaves other constructors able to establish locks before its raw descriptor closes. One shared opening boundary prevents that overlap without holding a process lock across cache work.
