## Packaging defect and corrected candidate

A raw-byte scan found no `/home`, the retained toolchain folder, `thinkthen_panic_probe`, `libssl` or `libcrypto` in the extracted extension. It did find **218 `/work` occurrences** in that extension, including `/work/work/crates/thinkthen/...` and `/work/cargo/registry/...`. The compressed gem bytes do not expose those paths until extraction. `release-pack` remaps `$HOME` but this build's source and isolated Cargo registry sit outside `$HOME`; `release-container` likewise archives source outside its home. The first artifact therefore failed the requested all-builder-path hygiene proof.

At committed source `b6a2d06ac9c2a9cbf15ae333c7afcf5880817f83`, `sdlc/scripts/release-pack` and `libraries/ruby/build.sh` compose path remaps for the repository, `CARGO_HOME` and `HOME` with any incoming flags. The Ruby script still appends Darwin's `-undefined dynamic_lookup` linker flag after setting `RUSTFLAGS`; the Linux build exercised the combined wrapper and direct-builder flags. A no-build Darwin `uname`/Cargo shim captured the preexisting flag, all three new remaps and `dynamic_lookup` together, exiting before artifact mutation. An actual current-source Darwin link remains for review and its native runner. ## What the build taught us

A source archive and Cargo registry may live outside HOME. Remapping HOME alone therefore missed real builder paths, and scanning a compressed gem hid them. Inspect the extracted native image and cover the actual source, registry and home roots while preserving inherited and platform linker flags. The installed full runner also exposed inherited default batching in eight old Ruby fixtures; record those failures and feed them to the Ruby batching preparation instead of rerunning the unchanged suite after a path-only correction.
