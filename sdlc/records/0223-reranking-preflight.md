## What the build taught us

This is a preparation record. Tracing the score decoder found that the vendor's own score is discarded, while `answer.level` and the weighted `value` answer different questions. Tracing the output row found two required changes, not just a new file-kind allowance: the ordered sink currently receives no sortable number from a score answer, and ranked details forcibly erase `value`. The accepted 0220 retention work supplies both bounds already. The binding inventory found a public yes-probability contract that should not be repurposed. Fresh design review must validate the CLI-first boundary, exact graded details, and honest deferral of optional weights and RRF. The eventual builder must report which preparation facts helped and which were missing.
