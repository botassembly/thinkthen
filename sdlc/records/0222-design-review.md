Review exact C++ registrations and overload compatibility first. The proposal adds context after an existing deadline on normal/listed scalars, rather than adding a same-arity VARCHAR overload that could disturb an untyped `NULL` deadline; warm takes its new third context. Check `NULL` context as absent, ordinary `NULL` inputs, try-details' nullable deadline and safe row failures, bind-time question/member validation, and execution-time caller settings. The accepted B0 final-context rule and vector-edge packing supply public authority; flag only a genuine new choice.

Follow a vector from `thinkthen.cpp::{Decide,Listed}` through `bridge/src/ffi/scalar/ffi.rs`, `ffi/listed.rs`, `src/engines.rs` and B12a's future many-record returns. A batch key needs complete ordered options and context, full encoded size, first-seen rows and original output slots. The corrected proposal includes `try_details` in `max` packing through an explicitly reviewed public Rust `Engine::details_many_recoverable_with` method reachable by the separate bridge crate, a private adapter reusing `core::Batcher`, a required shared one-split method around the current single-send `ask_batch`, and `workers::ordered`. The bridge reaches it through `thinkthen_cpp_try_details_group` across the native ABI. Check that recoverable whole-request errors map to that request's slots, and that a reply with good/failed/good logical members maps each slot separately while a later independent request still runs. Pin exact safe failed JSON, two request identities and two attempts; no isolation send. Invalid foldable prepared questions remain failed JSON beside good answers. ADR 0089's ordinary stopping stream stays unchanged. Check that `Kind::{Cancelled,Deadline,Defect}` leaves the worker as outer `Err` as soon as it completes, even if the older request is held; only recoverable Usage/Local/Backend errors may be wrapped for continuation. Existing attempts join and deliver in order, with no third admission after the fatal completion. One deterministic width-two acknowledgement witness is enough. Verify the shared adapter actually uses `halves_with_questions` once after eligible 413/`max_tokens_exceeded`, counts the refused attempt and both halves, and never treats the present `ask_batch` as split-aware. A temporary row-isolated stage cannot close B13c. Warm's sorted `std::set` must become first-seen owned state without relaxing its one-question-per-aggregate refusal. Check the paired `StatementOwner::Remaining` to C++/Rust warm ABI argument to `engines::options`; its zero-budget case must have an exact Deadline error and zero sends. Inspect `StatementOwner` and `SendBudget` together: query expiry spans vectors, a scalar deadline spans its own call, and actual retries/413 halves count as sends. No pre-send row truncation may stand in for attempt admission.
