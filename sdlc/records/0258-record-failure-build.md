The candidate scheduler matched that shared variant after `prepare_group` and could therefore skip a missing `--field`, contrary to ADR 0104. A private `PrepareError` now marks only a miss from `group_evidence` as `MissingOn`; field selection and all other failures retain their original `Failure` and terminal behavior. Dry run converts the private error back to the same existing diagnostic. No public surface, dependency, or engine scheduler changed.
