## What this preparation taught us

The corrected inventory saved the nested-grammar refusal and warned that the scheduler is concurrent. Fresh review of frozen `8121c171` found three omissions: backpressure had no oldest-row progress proof, the general multi-record sentence skipped structured question text, and the proposed metadata path omitted the hand-maintained result schema and J1 type corpus. The first miss came from listing resource bounds without tracing which event releases the oldest row; the other two came from stopping source/prose inventory at the planner and result constructor. It did not yet inventory `AnnotateMeta`/`RequestMeta`, `facade::split`, or `QuestionSet::from_parts`; those reveal the per-group metadata and singleton profile seams. The 0215 logical/wire finding also applies to multi-label annotate questions. The existing 182-case benchmark is keyed but has the wrong question kinds for B10's exact proof. The corrected design keeps at most 4,096 admitted rows plus one candidate, at most 4,096 × `Q` row/slice fragments for finite question count `Q`, and at most `--jobs` in-flight requests. It reads to an accepted close for every slice of the oldest row before applying backpressure, prioritizes that row on dispatch, and never closes for window fullness or a worker-induced pause. A stable oldest-row slice closes by the 4,096-member cap; a layout change or structured singleton closes earlier. This fixed window does not depend on `--jobs`, preserving file/fast-pipe body and digest identity.
