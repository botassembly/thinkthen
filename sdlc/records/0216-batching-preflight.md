## What this preparation taught us

The corrected inventory saved the nested-grammar refusal and warned that the scheduler is concurrent. It did not yet inventory `AnnotateMeta`/`RequestMeta`, `facade::split`, or `QuestionSet::from_parts`; those reveal the per-group metadata and singleton profile seams. The 0215 logical/wire finding also applies to multi-label annotate questions. The existing 182-case benchmark is keyed but has the wrong question kinds for B10's exact proof.
