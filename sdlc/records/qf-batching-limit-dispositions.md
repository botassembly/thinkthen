## What the assessment taught us

The three findings have different boundaries. Rank's raw sort is correct for its accepted algorithm, and the rank page now states its cross-batch meaning. The records page now names the repeated-copy edge of the two forms; changing the forms would alter compatible request bytes. The result page already names even token allocation; renaming its stable field would alter the public schema. Batch-one isolation is a request-shape property, not a calibration experiment. B6/D1's broader accuracy measurement remains separate.
