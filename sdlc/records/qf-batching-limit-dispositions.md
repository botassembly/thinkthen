## What the assessment taught us

The three findings have different boundaries. Rank's raw sort is correct for its accepted algorithm, and the rank page now states its cross-batch meaning. The records page now names the repeated-copy edge of the two forms; changing the forms would alter compatible request bytes. The result page documents even token allocation, but its field name does not meet 61's explicit naming criterion. The first disposition mistakenly treated documentation as enough and proposed non-issue despite that gap. The fresh Medium review accepted the 57 and 58 page corrections and caught this 61 error. A name change needs a separate public schema decision; none is made here. Batch-one isolation is a request-shape property, not a calibration experiment. B6/D1's broader accuracy measurement remains separate.
