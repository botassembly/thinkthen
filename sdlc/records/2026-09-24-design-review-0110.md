### 0110: FINDINGS

1. **The ticket does not say how the probe reads its result.** Opening `<folder>/.probe` for reading almost always fails. The spike told a refusal from an allowed folder by DuckDB's error type. A refused folder gives `DUCKDB_ERROR_PERMISSION` ("Cannot access file …" or "LocalFileSystem has been disabled"). An allowed folder gives `DUCKDB_ERROR_IO` ("No such file").
