`verify` would judge whether the text supports a claim. It likely covers qualification: whether a statement is negated, uncertain, or someone else's claim, with the plain statement as the claim.

The experiments repository's experiment 0007 tests that shape and holds the open questions.

The experiment also shows whether `verify` does a job that `decide` with a well-formed question cannot.
