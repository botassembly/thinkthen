## Ambiguities and how this index was built

Round 5 and wave 6 use their own item numbers (lens notes call things "item 12" or "finding-8," wave-6 claims use codes like S1, P1, D3, Q2, G6), not the R1 through R4 row IDs in this index. Matching them back to the original rows was done by reading each defect's description, not by a shared ID. Rows marked "not mentioned" are ones where no round-5 or wave-6 text could be tied to that specific row with confidence; their status is carried forward from round 4 and marked "(carried)." A few rows likely were touched by round 5 or wave 6 without a strong enough textual match to say so honestly; this favors under-claiming over over-claiming.

R4-1 (stand-in use-after-free) is marked Partial after wave 6: the specific race wave 5 closed is now confirmed clean under TSan, but a related crash at C-door churn still happens about 1 time in 83 runs and was not reproduced by the independent verifier, so the underlying defect family is not fully closed.

R4-16 and R4-22 (DuckDB) are marked Partial because wave 6 fixed part of what each row bundles (logical-type leaks; a chained-handler interrupt miss) while other parts of the same rows (deprecated API use and build warnings; the original multi-query cancel scenario) were not separately reconfirmed.

Two items carried from round 4 as genuinely unknown (R3-20, R3-25) stay unknown: round 5 and wave 6 do not mention them either.

Several round-4 items still show "OPEN not probed" language from round 4 itself; where round 5 or wave 6 supplied no new evidence, this index carries that status rather than guessing.

The R6 rows come from wave6-outcomes.md's "New findings from verification" section and the four verifier note files; all but one (R6-15, a resend bug that also exists in main's own CLI, owned by core) were fixed in the same wave.
