# Marketing copy breaks the approved vocabulary

Status: Open

The approved vocabulary (`products/thinkthen/vocabulary.md`, approved 2026-09-21) fixes one word per concept. The marketing pages and slides drafted on 2026-09-20 carry seven breaks. The fixes land in the marketing repository. This issue is the record.

| Break | Where | Fixed word |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "category" for what `choose` picks | `use-case-slides.md` line 27, `use-cases.md` lines 35 and 48 | option |
| "topics" for what `tag` tests | `use-cases.md` lines 108-109 | label |
| "buckets" beside band talk | `deck.md` line 76, `objections.md` line 30 | band, middle range |
| `.answer.confidence` taught on a slide | deck slide `09-find`, the `--details` example | one ruling, see below |
| the analogy in a headline | title slide, slide 02, `site.md` "The first screen" | "semantic commands" |
| "so `if` works" on a function slide | slide 03, end of the exit-code line | say what the function does |
| "the unsure middle" | slide 11 | band, not sure |

The confidence row is a ruling conflict rather than a banned word. The vendor field is real and `specification/result.md` keeps it, but `demos/FINDINGS.md` says to keep `confidence` out of every example until a measurement says what it is for. Either the measurement exists and that finding closes, or the slide drops the field.

The headline row breaks the vocabulary's placement rule: "Semantic commands" belongs in every headline, first sentence, and title, and the `if`/`grep`/`sort` analogy belongs in the talk's opening and the announcement's closing. The title slide, slide 02, and the site plan carry the analogy where the name belongs. The pages predate the vocabulary's approval.

## How bad it is for a user

Minor. The deck still teaches the right behavior, and the words blur it.
