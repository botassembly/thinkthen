This decision applies Ian's [0.2.1 contract ruling](2026-10-18-0-2-1-rebuilds-the-basis.md) and [freeze and qualification ruling](2026-10-20-freeze-names-the-contract.md).

Findings after the freeze go to the next release unless they block shipping. Shipping blockers that preserve the frozen contracts proceed as ordinary tickets alongside language work. A shipping blocker that changes a frozen contract still requires the ruling and language batch above.
