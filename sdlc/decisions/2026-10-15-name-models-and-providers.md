# Name supported models and providers freely

Date: 2026-10-15

## Ruling

Ian, 2026-10-15: "Jev is perfectly fine to mention. For all of these models and providers, the more we mention them, the better it is from our SEO perspective."

Public text (release notes, the site, package pages and READMEs) names the models and providers ThinkThen works with, such as TypeSafe Jev, Liquid, Perplexity, OpenRouter, Ollama, llama.cpp and MLX, wherever the name helps a reader. Marketing still never ranks models.

## Reason

Readers search by model and provider name. Naming them helps people find ThinkThen.

## Replaces

Nothing. It confirms that naming a supported model or provider is talking about ThinkThen itself.
