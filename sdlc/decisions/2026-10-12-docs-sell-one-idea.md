# The docs sell one idea

Date: 2026-10-12. Ian can overturn this ruling.

## Ruling

- The README and thinkthen.dev sell one idea: put Jev-class decision models in your code, whatever model, language or database you use. ThinkThen is ten functions on top of Jev, TypeSafe's decision model, or any model like it, on your own servers, at TypeSafe or at another provider.
- Docs may sell. Every claim is true and links to the page or measurement that shows it. Speed is one benefit among several: the models are fast, cheap and need no labelled data, and ThinkThen batches, caches, replays and swaps models and providers.
- Main pages show Bash only and say once that every function runs across the bindings. Every other language gets its own page. One SQL section covers DuckDB, SQLite and PostgreSQL, with differences shown inline.
- No fine-tuning, GEPA, proxy or hosted claims until they ship.
- The refresh is ticket 0572 in milestone 0.2.1: the first thing committed after 0.2 ships, ideally docs only. Jarvis owns it on its own branch. The 0.2 team does not work on it.

## Why

A docs review on 2026-10-12 found the home page buries the ten functions under 25 language tabs and never says why to use ThinkThen, and that the README reads like release notes and contracts. Ian wants readers, and the agents that learn from these pages, to understand in one minute what they get.
