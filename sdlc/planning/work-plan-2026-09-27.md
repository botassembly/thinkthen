## The batches

A batch is related tickets that touch the same files, handed to one builder that keeps its context and builds them in order. Keep that builder for the next related ticket instead of starting a new one. Fresh read-only reviewers independently review the designs and completed changes; authors never approve their own code review. The owning coordinator accepts, lands and pushes. Each lane has its own worktree, and every heavy build takes its turn under the shared lock.

Prepare the next related ticket family when Ian requests it; otherwise prepare one ready ticket ahead of each builder. Record this preparation with the ticket or its build record. Keep dependency and support limits explicit. Do not research the whole queue far ahead, duplicate settled experiments, or require a full gate for the preparation. For database-family preparation, inspect the existing code, add concrete code and problem notes, and preserve the ticket's intended outcomes. After preparation, review its accuracy and usefulness; after each completed ticket, record what it helped or missed and use that evidence to improve the next preparation brief.
