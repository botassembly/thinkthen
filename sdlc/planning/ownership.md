Ian ruled on 2026-09-26 that two agents share this repository. The marketing lead owns `site/`, `.github/workflows/pages.yml`, and `.claude/skills/site-writing/`, and lands those changes on main itself. It also owns the slides and Beatles Bench, which live in other repositories. The queue owner owns everything else: the code, `specification/`, `spec/`, `demos/`, `sdlc/`, the rungs, and this file. A one-line pointer to `site/` in this file is the one exception.
