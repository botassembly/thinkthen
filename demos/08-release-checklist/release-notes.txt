Release 4.3.0

What changed
- The CSV export now writes a header row on every report.
- Upload limit raised from 100 MB to 512 MB.
- The session cookie lifetime drops from 12 hours to 2 hours.

Upgrade
Run `app migrate` before starting the new build. The migration adds one
index and takes under a minute on a million rows.

Known issues
The dark theme still ignores the print stylesheet.
